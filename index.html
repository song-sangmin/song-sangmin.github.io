Research Interests

My dissertation explores new ways of modeling carbon exchange between the ocean and atmosphere by leveraging observations from a growing, global fleet of drifting oceanic robots called Argo floats. Our regression model, CRUSOE, is a novel machine learning approach to reconstructing the variability of carbon uptake in the dynamic Southern Ocean (i.e. the region around Antarctica) each year. Incredibly, this region is thought to make up around 40% of global oceanic uptake but remains difficult to observe and understand due to its remote location and harsh conditions. The novel surface ocean pCO2 product generated by CRUSOE will provide a key missing piece for improving the quantification of the Southern Ocean carbon sink, which will ultimately be essential for reducing uncertainties in the Global Carbon Budget.
