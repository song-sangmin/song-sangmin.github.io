Research

My research explores carbon exchange between the ocean and atmosphere by leveraging observations from a growing, global fleet of drifting oceanic robots called [Argo floats]. More specifically, I am interested in improving estimates of how much anthropogenic carbon is taken up by the Southern Ocean (i.e. the region around Antarctica) each year. Incredibly, this region is thought to make up around [40% of global oceanic uptake] but remains difficult to observe and understand due to its remote location and harsh conditions.

To achieve better mapping of surface carbon despite these data limitations, I am currently developing a novel machine learning framework that merges large ship and Argo float datasets and applies a two-step clustering and regression approach. >
